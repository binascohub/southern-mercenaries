The hero models are assembled from the three Quaternius packs above by `tools/assets/build_heroes.py` (Blender as a Python module), from the original packs kept in `uploads/`: textures reduced to 1024 px, only the head of the base characters kept under the outfits. The village and prop pieces are exported one by one by `tools/assets/export_kit.py` (shared textures as 1024 px WebP).

The Sewer monsters are converted from the original .blend files by `tools/assets/build_monsters.py`: only the game's mesh, skeleton and animations kept (idle, walk, run, attack, hit, die; the bat's flight and death), textures re-linked to the materials and reduced to 512 px WebP, size normalized (rat 1 m, gator 3 m), the rat's hair cards left out. The bat is modified from the original (CC BY-SA 4.0): only the regular bat kept, textures reduced.
